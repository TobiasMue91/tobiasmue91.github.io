Welcome to the Thermoverse

Temperature conversion, with a little kinetic energy.

Type any value or drag the spectrum. Every scale updates at once, the background changes with the thermal mood, and the particles speed up as things get hotter.

All conversions pass through Celsius as a canonical intermediate. Values below absolute zero are rejected because even playful tools should respect thermodynamics.

Scale Reference
Celsius Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C.
Fahrenheit Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F.
Kelvin An absolute scale beginning at 0 K.
Rankine Absolute temperature using Fahrenheit-sized degrees.
Réaumur Historic scale: water boils at 80°Ré.
Newton Historic scale: water boils at 33°N.
