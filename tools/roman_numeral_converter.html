    • Write the thousands, then hundreds, tens and ones: 1994 = M + CM + XC + IV.
    • A smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted, in six pairs only: IV 4, IX 9, XL 40, XC 90, CD 400, CM 900.
    • I, X, C, M repeat up to three times; V, L, D never repeat.
    • Clocks and old inscriptions often write 4 as IIII and 9 as VIIII. This page reads them and says so.
    • There is no zero. Above 3,999 a bar multiplies by 1,000: V is 5,000.
