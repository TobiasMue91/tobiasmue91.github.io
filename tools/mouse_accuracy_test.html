What the four numbers mean

Accuracy is the share of clicks that landed inside the target ring. Mean error is the average distance from the exact centre — it keeps counting even when you hit, which is why it is the more honest number of the two. Underneath it you get the same figure as a share of the target radius, which is the one number that survives a change of target size: 50% means your typical click sits halfway between centre and edge, whether the target was wide or tiny. Group size is the radius of the circle holding your closest half of all clicks; it describes how tightly you shoot, independent of where you are aiming. Avg. per target is timing information only, shown so you can see what you traded for the precision.

Why the direction matters

Almost nobody scatters evenly. Most people sit slightly low and to one side of centre, because a hand pivots around the wrist rather than sliding straight. A tight group in the wrong place is an aiming problem you can correct in a few minutes; a wide group centred perfectly is a grip, sensitivity or surface problem. The plot separates the two.

Long jumps versus short ones

The distance to the next target is deliberately varied. Long travel is where pointer acceleration, a cramped mousepad or a too-high sensitivity show themselves: the hand arrives fast and overshoots. If your long-jump error is much worse than your short-jump error, the fix is usually sensitivity or desk space rather than practice. With twenty targets that split rests on ten clicks per half, so the run only mentions it when the gap is large enough to be worth acting on — the forty-target run gives it a firmer footing.

A note on the pixels

All distances are CSS pixels in this window, so a run on a laptop trackpad and a run on a 27-inch screen are not comparable, and neither are two runs at very different window sizes. Compare yourself to your own earlier runs in the same mode — that is what the history strip is for. Nothing is uploaded: the test runs entirely in your browser and your results are stored locally on this device.
