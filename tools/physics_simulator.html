How it’s modelled

A world 8 m wide and 5 m tall, the same on every screen, in SI units. Shapes are flat and weigh 100 kg per m², so a ball of radius 0.3 m weighs 28.3 kg. Time moves in steps of 1/240 s whatever your screen’s refresh rate. When two shapes meet, the bouncier of the two sets the bounce and friction is the geometric mean of theirs (√(μ₁·μ₂)); a rolling ball meets a little rolling resistance (coefficient 0.01), so it stops in the end. That is why a ball rolling down a slope arrives about 1% slower than √(10gh/7); set friction to 0 and it slides without rolling, at √(2gh).
