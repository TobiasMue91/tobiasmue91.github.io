Air Synth

Hold a hand up to the camera and play. Whatever you do stays in time and in key; how much you move decides how big the band plays.

✋ up and downthe note, always one from the chord ✋ left and rightdarker to brighter ✌ two fingersan arpeggio ✊ fistthe beat drops out; open to bring it back 🤏 pinchthe next chord ✋✋ second handhigher is more echo and space

The camera picture is analysed on this device and never uploaded. The hand model (about 8 MB) loads once; you can play with the mouse while it does.

How to play

✋ heightthe note - the lines are the chord's notes ✋ left / rightfilter, darker to brighter move fastlouder, and the band joins in ✌ two fingersarpeggio (A, or the Arp button) ✊ fistbreak (hold B); open for the drop 🤏 pinchnext chord (Space) second handecho and space (mouse wheel, second finger) Looprecords four bars, press again to layer; hold to clear Recsaves a video of your performance with sound
