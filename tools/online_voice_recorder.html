    Notes

    Nothing is uploaded

    Recording, editing and export all happen inside this tab. Takes live in your browser's own database on this device, so clearing site data removes them.

    Formats

    Recording uses the best format your browser offers — usually WebM / Opus, or MP4 / AAC on Safari. Export to WAV for something every editor can open.

    Good levels

    Aim for peaks between −12 and −6 dBFS. If the clip light comes on, pull the gain down — clipped audio cannot be repaired afterwards.

    Editing

    Drag across the waveform to select, scroll to zoom. Trim, normalise, fade and reverse all write a new WAV in place, and every step can be undone.
