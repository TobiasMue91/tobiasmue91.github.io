Settings: keep photo orientation and colour profile
How it works: hidden details are left out of a copy, which is then checked again. Pictures and sound are not re-compressed; PDFs are rewritten with their pages kept.

Each file's structure is read in your browser. Its metadata blocks (Exif, GPS, XMP, IPTC, comments, ID3 and other tags, PDF document info, comment authors and earlier revisions) are left out of the copy, and the picture or sound data is copied byte for byte, so nothing is re-compressed. The copy is then read again to check that nothing is left; you can confirm it with exiftool or your computer's file-info panel. Video and HEIC metadata is overwritten with zeros in place, so the file keeps its size. Nothing is kept after you close the page.
