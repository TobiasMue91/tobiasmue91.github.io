    How To Use

    1. Create a plan and give it a name. You get a link; that link is the plan.
    2. Send the link to whoever shares the chores. Anyone with it sees the same list, live.
    3. Add a task and say how often it comes round — daily, weekly, monthly, or every N days.
    4. Tap a task's status to tick it off. It goes green until it next falls due, then comes back.
    5. The list is ordered by what is furthest past due, so the top of it is what to do next.
    6. Tap a task's name, frequency or date to change it without losing its history.
    7. This browser remembers the plans you have opened, so you do not have to keep the link.
