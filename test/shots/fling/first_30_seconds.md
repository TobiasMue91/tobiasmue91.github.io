## The first 30 seconds (written before the code)

**Phone, first visit.** The page opens straight on level 1, no menu. Sky, violet paper hills, a sling at lower left, one finch in it,
two more waiting on the grass, one wooden hut with one toad in it. A cream ghost finger rests on the bird and slides back-and-down,
dotted arc growing as it goes, then lets go. (0-3 s: the hint loops until the player touches the screen.)
- **Touch** near the bird: the ghost vanishes, the bird follows the thumb, the band stretches, six tapered dots show the first
  half-second of flight, the pouch creaks (pitch rises with the pull). Drag too short and let go: bird returns, nothing is spent.
- **Release:** twang, the bird flies, the camera follows. The hut cracks (cracks are drawn on a block as it weakens), planks tumble,
  the toad pops with a paper burst and "+5,000". On the last toad the world slows for a beat, then spare birds are counted
  one by one (+10,000 each) and the card drops in: stars stamp one at a time, Next is the big button.
- **First mistake** (a miss): the bird rolls, fades, the next hops onto the sling. A faint echo of the last shot's aim stays on the
  screen as dots, so the second try is an adjustment, not a new guess. No text, no penalty beyond the bird.
- **Out of birds:** the card says how many toads are left; Retry is the big button (also R). One tap and you are aiming again.
- **Ability birds** (level 4+): while in flight a pulsing ring sits on the bird; any tap uses it. Level 4's yellow bird is the lesson.
- **Tomorrow:** the page opens on the first level that is not yet cleared; stars stay. The map (hamburger) shows every level.

**Screens and exits.** intro pan (touch skips) -> aim -> flight (tap = ability) -> watch (wait for quiet) -> aim again | win | lose.
win: card (Next / Retry / Map). lose: card (Retry / Map). Map: grid of paper tags, locked levels dimmed, tap to play, Esc/close returns.
Phone landscape/portrait both work; portrait pans, drag anywhere away from the sling to look around.
