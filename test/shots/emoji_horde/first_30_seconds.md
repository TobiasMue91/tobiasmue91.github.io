## The first 30 seconds (written before the code)

**Phone, first visit.** A dark field. A pristine heart sits in the middle, not beating, a faint glow breathing around it.
Title "Emoji Horde", one line ("Keep your heart beating."), one big gold button: Begin. (The tap also unlocks audio.)
- **0:02 Begin.** The title lifts away; the heart gives its first lub-dub by itself. The hint "Tap to beat" fades in under it.
- **0:04** One grump walks in from the top (the first wave is three slow grumps that come from the top and start close, so nobody waits).
- **0:08 First tap, anywhere.** The heart squashes and rebounds, a gold ring leaves it, the grump squints (white rim), swells, goes white and
  bursts into shards and coins, the coins fly to the heart on arcs, the bank counts up and bumps, a glass note plays. Nothing on screen but
  the wave, the heart and the love count. A grump dies to exactly one thump, so the first three taps are three kills.
- **0:16 Wave 1 clears.** The bar fills, "Wave 2" pops in, and the heart starts to beat on its own (50 bpm). A pill says "It beats by itself now".
  This is the idle promise, taught by it happening.
- **0:25 Love reaches half the price of the first card.** The shop panel opens (eased: the heart glides up, it never jumps) and the first
  card slides in: Thump, glowing when affordable. A dashed tile with a "?" is the next one to come. Tap: the card bumps, a gold ring leaves
  the heart, sparks, and "1.2 thump" floats up. The next pulse is visibly bigger.
- **0:30** Pods of grumps arrive. A pulse catches three at once: a rising three-note chime in A-minor pentatonic, three pops, one tally "+N".
  The player knows: tap = extra beat, love = cards, cards make the ring bigger. No text explained any of it.

**First mistake.** Ignoring the heart. Faces cling on and bite; the heart's blood level IS the health bar (it drains from the top), the screen
edges go red, the beat gets ragged, the phone buzzes. A tap peels every clinging face off. At zero: **Heartbreak** - the heart cracks white,
the crowd drains away, one calm line ("Back to wave N. Your love is safe."), two seconds later the blood refills and the game carries on two
waves lower. Never a game-over screen.

**The end of a session.** Nothing ends. The shop is the to-do list. At wave 25 a gold Mend chip appears under the wave counter: mending pours
gold into the cracks (a permanent seam on the heart: love and thump +40% each), and starts again from wave 1.

**Tomorrow.** The page opens on "Welcome back" with the heart, "While you were away (3 hours) your heart held on +380K", one button: Collect.
Time away pays 40% of the earning rate, up to eight hours, and the veil is also what unlocks audio.

**Screens and exits.** Title (new save) -> Begin | Welcome back (save, or a return after a minute away) -> Collect/Continue | play (tap = beat;
cards buy on tap, repeat on hold; shop rail on phones, dock on desktops) | boss wave (banner + horn + red edge + a marker where it will come in)
| heartbreak (two seconds, automatic) | settings sheet (cog: sound, vibration, hold to start over, Done / Esc) | Mend sheet (chip: before/after
hearts, hold to mend, Not yet / Esc). The sim pauses behind every sheet.
