Underfoot · Tempo

A prototype. Each level sends you out for something, and taking it turns the level round: home again, against a clock, rising water or a bank that falls away behind you. Keep running and you get faster, twice: fast enough to burst clods and bowl beetles over. Stop, turn round or get knocked, and it is gone.
