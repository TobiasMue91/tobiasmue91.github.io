Underfoot · Tempo

A prototype. Run out to the seed sack at the far end of the garden, then home to the mouse hole before the clock runs out. Keep running and you get faster, twice: fast enough to burst clods and bowl beetles over. Stop, turn round or get knocked, and it is gone.

Moves

Faster keep running without a stop or a turn: the trail behind you turns orange, then red · Slide down while running: it keeps your speed for as long as you hold it, and under anything low · Wall kick jump at a wall, and jump again while you touch it
