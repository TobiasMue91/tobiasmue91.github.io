The hundredth gameMenagerie

Nobody designs the animals here. They are chains of bone and muscle, thrown together at random, and the ones that get furthest across your world become the parents of the next generation. Give it a minute and something will be walking.

  1. Sculpt the ground — drag to raise hills, dig pits, lower a roof they must crawl under, or ice the floor.
  2. Watch them try. The leader is picked out in amber; the rest are ghosts.
  3. Keep the ones you like on the shelf, then drop them into a world they were never bred for.

A thing bred for hills is helpless in water. That is rather the point.

Everything here runs on your device. Muscles are power-limited, so nothing can cheat its way across by brute force — it has to actually learn to move.

The shelf

Animals you kept. Release drops one into the world you have open now — it will be judged against ground it never grew up on.
