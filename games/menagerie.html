Drag on the world to sculpt it. The animals are bred against whatever you leave behind.

The hundredth gameMenagerie

Nobody designs the animals here. Each one is a back with legs hung off it — how many legs, how long, how they are timed, all of it thrown together at random — and the ones that get furthest across your world become the parents of the next generation. Give it a minute and something will be walking.

  1. Sculpt the ground — drag to raise hills, dig pits, lower a roof they must crawl under, or ice the floor.
  2. Watch them try. The leader is picked out in amber; the rest are ghosts. Its name and the line under it say what it has become — Quadrupes has four legs.
  3. Keep the ones you like on the shelf, then drop them into a world they were never bred for.

A thing bred for hills is helpless in water. That is rather the point.

Everything here runs on your device. Muscles are power-limited and a belly grips the ground as hard as a foot does, so nothing can slide or brute-force its way across — it has to get up on its legs and walk.

The shelf

Animals you kept. Release drops one into the world you have open now — it will be judged against ground it never grew up on.
