How scoring works

Roll up to three times a turn. Tap dice to keep them between rolls, then put the dice in one empty box. Thirteen turns fill the card.

Ones to Sixes score those dice only. Reach 63 on that side (three of each) for a +35 bonus.

3 / 4 of a kind and Chance score every die. Full house 25, small straight (four in a row) 30, large straight 40, Yacht (five alike) 50.

Each extra Yacht after a 50 is worth +100 and must go in its number's box if that is empty; if not, any box on the right takes it at full value.

No box fits? Every turn must fill one, even for nothing.
