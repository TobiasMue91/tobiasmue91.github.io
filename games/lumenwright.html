How light works here

Every detector wants one exact colour. Turn the optics until they all read true — and don't spill anything extra into them.

  1. 1Click any piece to turn it. Right-click, or hold Shift, to turn it the other way. Emitters, walls, filters and detectors are bolted down.
  2. 2Colour adds up. A red beam and a green beam arriving at the same detector make yellow. All three primaries make white.
  3. 3Exactly means exactly. A cyan detector fed cyan and a stray red reads white, and stays dark.
  4. 4Par is real. It is the shortest solution the generator could prove, so matching it is a genuine result.
