A Deck That Never ShufflesProcession

Nine fights. One new card after each. The usual bargain — except that your deck is never shuffled.

At the end of every turn the cards you played go to the bottom of the deck in the order you played them, and the cards you didn't play follow behind. Nothing is random. You can see what is coming. So every turn you are choosing two things at once: what happens now, and what you will be holding three turns from now.

The consequence worth knowing early: cards you play together come back together. Fire off three attacks in a row and in two turns' time you will draw a fistful of attacks and nothing to block with. Spacing your plays is as much of the game as choosing them.

Enemies announce their next move exactly. Anything that kills you was something you could see.

Energy is 3 a turn and unspent energy is lost. Block soaks damage and is gone at the start of your next turn. Strength adds to every hit of an attack, so a card that hits four times gains four times as much. Poison takes its value off the enemy at end of turn, then drops by one. Thorns hurt anything that hits you. Four kinds of card — force, guard, venom and tempo — each have one card that pays off enormously if you built for it and does nearly nothing if you didn't.
