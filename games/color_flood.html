How to play Color Flood

Color Flood is a flood-fill puzzle. You always flood from the top-left corner. Pick one of the colours and your whole connected region turns that colour and swallows every touching cell of that colour. Flood the entire board with a single colour in as few moves as you can. Beat par for three stars. There are forty levels from small ponds to large oceans, and a new board every day.
