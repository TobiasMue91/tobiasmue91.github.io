Blobby Volley

The ball leaves a blob at the same speed every single time — only the direction changes, and that is decided by which part of you it hits. Serve-side scoring to 15, two clear points, three touches a side.

Move with left/right, jump with up — hold the jump key to go higher.
Mouse steers the blob towards the pointer; hold the left button to jump.
On a phone you get buttons for each blob — and the court itself still works: drag your half to walk there, tap it to jump. Turn the phone sideways for a bigger court.

Physics ported from the open-source Blobby Volley 2 constants: blob speed 4.5, jump 15.1, ball gravity 0.287, bounce 13.12.
