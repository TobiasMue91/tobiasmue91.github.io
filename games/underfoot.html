Underfoot

A mouse, a garden, and the quick way home

How to play

Every level sends you out for something. Taking it turns the level round, and the way home is against something: a clock, rising water, a bank falling away, a mower, a cat. Home fast enough for S, A or B.

Moves

Speed
Keep running and you get faster, twice: the trail behind you turns orange, then red. That is fast enough to burst clods and bowl beetles over. A turn or a moment's stop keeps it; slow down for longer and it ebbs away a step at a time; a crash into a wall or a knock takes it all.
Slide
Down while running. It keeps your speed for as long as you hold it, and under anything low.
Wall kick
Jump at a wall, and jump again while you touch it. Two walls close together are a way up.
Mushroom
Run over one with jump held, and it throws you high, at full speed.
Beetles
Jump on them, bump them from below, or run them down at speed. Hits in a row make a combo.

Controls

Keyboard ← → or A D move · Space Z K or ↑ jump · ↓ slide · Shift X run, when always run is off · R restart · P pause · Esc menu

Gamepad stick or d-pad move, down slides · A jump · Start menu
