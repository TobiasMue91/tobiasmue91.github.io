Tap to drop the penguin

Pingu Throw

Tap once and the penguin dives off the ice wall. Tap again and the yeti swings — the club comes through the moment you press, so the only question is how level the penguin is with it. Dead level sends it furthest; a bit high lobs it, a bit low skids it. The dashed line is the club's height.

Keyboard: space to drop, space to swing.
